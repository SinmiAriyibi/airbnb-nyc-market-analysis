# Airbnb NYC Market Dashboard (Tableau)

I built an interactive Tableau dashboard using a Kaggle NYC Airbnb dataset to analyze **pricing, demand signals, and listing distribution** across boroughs and neighborhoods. The goal was to turn raw listing data into **market intelligence** that supports real business decisions (pricing strategy, investment decisions, and neighborhood targeting).

 **Tools:** Tableau · Calculated Fields · Data Cleaning · KPI Design · Market Segmentation · Data Storytelling  
 **Source:** Kaggle (NYC Airbnb listings dataset)

## What I Built!
A Tableau dashboard that answers:
- Which of the NYC boroughs dominate Airbnb supply?
- What neighborhoods have the highest prices?
- What room types drive the market?
- Where are listings concentrated geographically?
- What does the price distribution look like (and where are the outliers)?

---

## What I Did (The Work Behind the Dashboard)
- Cleaned and standardized Kaggle Airbnb listing data for analysis readiness  
- Resolved inconsistent neighborhood labels and removed invalid/duplicate records  
- Created calculated fields to track KPI performance (price, availability, review activity)  
- Designed a clean dashboard layout optimized for quick stakeholder interpretation  
- Built interactive visuals for borough-level segmentation and geographic exploration

---

## Insights That Matter
- **Manhattan leads** in both listing volume and average price → premium pricing market
- **Brooklyn ranks high in supply** with lower average pricing → competitive market with strong demand
- **Availability is higher** in outer boroughs (Bronx/Staten Island) → weaker demand or oversupply
- **Private rooms + entire homes** dominate listings and reviews → strongest demand segments
- Price distribution is **heavily right-skewed** → most listings cluster below a threshold with a luxury tail

---

## Why This Matters (The Business Value)
This dashboard can help:
- Hosts set location-based pricing strategies  
- Investors identify high-demand neighborhoods  
- Operators track supply saturation and engagement trends  
- Teams spot pricing outliers and market inefficiencies
